Myth Box — Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Myth Box has no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. Unless you join Online, it sends nothing about you or your play anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your progress, streaks, relics and settings, such as sound and music volume, are saved on your own device so that the game remembers them. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Online

Online is optional. Nothing is sent until you open the Online tab, choose a name and press Join. Joining makes an anonymous account, with no email address and no password. Your device holds the only key to it.

While you are joined, the game's server keeps the name you chose and, for each daily puzzle you finish, the day, the world and the time you took. Other players see your name and your times on the daily leaderboards. Daily times are deleted after 30 days. The server is hosted by Supabase.

To delete everything, press Leave online at the foot of the Online tab. Your account, your name and your times are deleted from the server at once. If you uninstall the game without leaving, write to the developer and they will be deleted for you.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result, the game copies a short text, ending with a link to the game's Google Play page, to your clipboard. It holds no personal information, and it goes nowhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Children

Online asks for nothing but a name, and shows it to other players. Children should choose one that is not their real name.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the game does anything differently. For questions, write to the developer through the contact details on the game's Google Play page.
